Jonathan Distler is an undergraduate student in Mechanical Engineering. His research interests include biologically inspired robotics, soft robotics, human-robot interactions, and autonomous controls. Within the lab, he has worked on classical planning algorithms for LLM-mediated human-robot conversations, with his main focus having been on developing the robot's hardware controller.
