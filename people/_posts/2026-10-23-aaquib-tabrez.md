Aaquib Tabrez is a Postdoctoral Associate in the HRC² Lab. His research interests include human-robot interaction, explainable AI, reinforcement learning, and trustworthy autonomous systems. At HRC², Aaquib studies how foundation models fail and how humans and robots can recover from these failures during interaction.
